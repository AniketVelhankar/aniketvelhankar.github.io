## Hey, I am Aniket

### Deep Learning & Computer Vision Researcher

I'm a computer science researcher and developer passionate about **deep learning** and **computer vision**. Currently a final-year undergraduate student at [Pune Institute of Computer Technology (P.I.C.T)](https://pict.edu), where I'm exploring innovative applications of neural networks and visual computing.

My mission is to **bridge the gap between research and real-world applications** — transforming cutting-edge research ideas into practical solutions that solve meaningful problems. I'm particularly interested in:

- **Deep Learning & Neural Networks** — architecture design and optimization
- **Computer Vision** — object detection, image segmentation, and visual understanding
- **Applied Research** — translating academic concepts into production systems
- **Socially Relevant Applications** — using AI/ML to solve real-world challenges

I'm actively seeking challenging problems in **applied research** where I can leverage my problem-solving skills to build impactful real-world applications.
